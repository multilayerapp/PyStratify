## Near fields

Generated by the same script. max |F_ours - F_theirs| / |F_ours| over the points of a region: the
middle of the core and of every shell, and 1.05, 1.5, 3 outer radii in the host,
each at polar angles 0, 30, 90, 150, 180 deg and azimuth 40 deg; H compared as
scattnlay's H Z0 against PyStratify's H / n_host; nan = scattnlay returned nan at a point. Both
codes keep the near-field truncation (Allardice & Le Ru) + 20 orders. The treams columns are the
scattered field alone in the host, where treams' T-matrix fits (l_max <= 40); its H is in
PyStratify's units (H_inc = n_host), so it is compared directly. At the centre PyStratify is
exact (l = 1 only) and matches Bohren & Huffman's d_1 and m c_1 (BH, homogeneous spheres); scattnlay's
own fields lose digits as k r -> 0, hence its separate centre columns. - = not applicable.
